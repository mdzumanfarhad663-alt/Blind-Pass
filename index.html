Welcome To Blind Pass

Two buildings. One tropical courtyard. Zero rush.

Blind Pass Resort is a 25-room boutique motel where coastal relaxation meets classic charm, steps from St. Pete Beach. Two buildings surround a tropical courtyard with fountains.

Spend the day on the sand or close to home. Either way, expect personalized service and a relaxed, homey feel.

Highlights Of Your Stay

Heated Pool & Courtyard

Swim in the heated outdoor pool, then settle in with lounge chairs, tables and fountains close by.

Kitchenettes & Suites Up To 5 Guests

Most rooms have a kitchenette with a stovetop and sink, and our largest layout sleeps five.

Pet-Friendly Rooms

Bring your pet in select rooms. Pet fees apply and are discounted for longer stays.

Free Parking & Island Shuttle

Parking is free, and so is the beach shuttle that runs around the barrier island.
