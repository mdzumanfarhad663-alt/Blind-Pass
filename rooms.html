Good To Know

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 3 PM and check-out is at 11 AM.

Is parking free?

Yes, parking is free.

Do rooms have kitchens?

Most rooms have a kitchenette with a stovetop and sink. The Queen Studio has a mini-fridge and microwave.

Is there an elevator?

No. The King Suite with Spa Tub is on the second floor, and there is no elevator.
