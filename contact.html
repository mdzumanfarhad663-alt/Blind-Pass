Frequently Asked Questions

Your Stay

Do you allow pets?

Yes, in select rooms. Pet fees apply and are discounted for longer stays.

Is there an elevator, and do you have accessible rooms?

There is no elevator, and the King Suite with Spa Tub is on the second floor. We have one ADA King Room with a roll-in shower and grab bars.

Do rooms have kitchens?

Most rooms have a kitchenette with a stovetop and sink. The Queen Studio has a mini-fridge and microwave.

How far is the beach?

A short walk to the Gulf.
